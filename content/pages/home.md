The **Mile High Disc Golf Club (MHDGC)** is a community of players dedicated to growing and supporting disc golf across the Denver metro area and beyond. Whether you're brand new to the sport or a seasoned competitor, our club offers opportunities to play, compete, volunteer, and connect with fellow disc golfers.
