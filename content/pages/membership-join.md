---
title: How to Join
description: Come to any tag match to buy a tag and become a club member.
---

**Come to any tag match.** The tagmaster has tags for sale, and buying a tag makes you a member.

**Protected divisions: Ladies, Juniors and Grand Masters (55+).**

- You get your division tag **at no cost**, in addition to the general club tag.
- You get the **general club tag at a discount**.
- If the tagmaster doesn't have your division's tag on hand, we'll make arrangements to get you one. Reach us on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/groups/mhdgc/).
