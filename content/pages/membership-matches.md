---
title: How Do Tag Matches Work?
description: Check in, turn in your tags, play the round, and choose your next tag in score order.
---

Tag matches are rounds where club members compete to earn better tag numbers.

Here’s how a typical tag match works:

1. **Check In**
   Players usually sign in on UDisc and check in with one of the club’s Tag Masters.
2. **Tags Are Collected**
   Before the round begins, the Tag Master collects everyone’s tags.
3. **Play the Round**
   Players complete the round like a normal round of disc golf.
4. **Tags Are Reassigned**
   At the end of the round, the Tag Master calls players up in order of lowest score to highest score. Players then choose a tag in that order — meaning the lowest score receives the lowest available tag number.

For club tag scoring, **Ladies, Junior, and Grand Masters players typically receive a -3 stroke adjustment** when their scores are evaluated for the general club tag pool.
