---

Your bag tag is your membership to the Mile High Disc Golf Club.

Tags are a fun way to represent your membership in the Mile High Disc Golf Club and participate in friendly competition with other members.

When you join the club, you receive a numbered bag tag. The number represents your current ranking in the club — lower numbers are better.

Players can attach their tags to their disc golf bags as a way to show their current rank and earn some bragging rights around the course.

Our tags are also color-coded, usually grouped by number ranges (typically every 100 tags share a color). For example:

- Gold tags – lowest numbers
- Other colors (such as green and others) – higher numbered tags

The club offers multiple tag pools so players can compete with others in similar divisions:

- **MHDGC Club Tags** (open to all members)
- **Ladies Tags**
- **Grand Masters Tags**
- **Junior Tags**

Players participating in Ladies, Junior, or Grand Masters tags also play their Mile High Disc Golf Club tag at the same time.
