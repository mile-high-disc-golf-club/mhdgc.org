- **Weekly Tag Matches**
  Friendly competition where members battle for better bag tag numbers and bragging rights
- **Events & Tournaments**
  From casual leagues to sanctioned tournaments and charity events
- **Course Cleanups & Improvements**
  Helping maintain and improve the courses we love
- **Community Building**
  Welcoming players of all skill levels into the Colorado disc golf scene
