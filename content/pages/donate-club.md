---
title: Donating to the Club
description: How to donate directly to the Mile High Disc Golf Club.
---

We're working on a way to donate directly to the club, but we don't have a simple way to do that yet. If you'd like to give to the club directly, fill out our [contact form](/about#contact-us). In the meantime, or as an alternative, you can donate to our Winter Warriors / Ice Bowl fundraiser on Food Bank of the Rockies.
